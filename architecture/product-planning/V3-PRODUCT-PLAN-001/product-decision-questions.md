## Cross-capability decisions

- Which exact Customer create/update/search/retrieve operations form the first development slice? What minimum eligibility/quota/report/attachment mappings apply? Do not silently shrink first production scope.
- Which actor/action/resource rules, data classification, retention/legal obligations and disclosure-safe error policies are approved? Candidate DC/SC/RP/BC models are not defaults.
- Who owns each final Report Set, Feature Assignment/Plan/Subscription fact, Quota integration, Notification type, Attachment relation and privileged action? Which measurable examples establish acceptance?
- For Customer mutations, approve exact operation-specific failure policies only after DEC-014 durability, evidence-state separation, recovery/visibility, integrity/provenance and Sovereign-local safeguards have evidence. No automatic DURABLE_DEFER or fallback.
- What mandatory Audit events, sensitive Audit access/lifecycle operations, audit-of-audit recursion and durable-ingestion proof are required? Preserve already approved Customer and privileged obligations without inventing universal coverage.
- What critical-local catalog and profile-specific objectives should be assessed? Offline remains PROHIBITED; Air Gap is not a first-release implied feature. Numeric targets remain OD-023 OPEN.
- Approve the first documentation/design package separately from any IAM, FE-BE, Runtime remediation, implementation, CI or production task. Identify scope, exact prerequisites, allowed changes and evidence.
