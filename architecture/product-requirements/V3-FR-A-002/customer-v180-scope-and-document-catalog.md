## Current v1.8.0 user-confirmed decisions

The following refinements govern current scope over historical v1.7.0 OPEN statements. No formal acceptance or implementation authorization.

- **CM1-PD-96 — Actor operation and disclosure matrix:** Operator: ordinary customer create/update, Branch/Relationship management and general read; independent explicit authority for duplicate exception, activation/deactivation, archive/restore. Reviewer: general read; Catalog Manager: selectable roles and own-Tenant role catalog; Verifier: general read and verification, separate authority for revocation/evidence; Audit Reviewer: authorized Audit review only. Operator/Reviewer/Catalog Manager may view selectable roles. General customer read includes contact and verification status; official identifiers masked absent independent identity-field access; Branch/Relationship require relevant resource access; evidence content and business history require independent authority; Audit access separately authorized. Deny by default, no foreign-Tenant disclosure, warnings limited to authorized facts, Backend authorization mandatory; no IAM grants.
- **CM1-PD-97 — Profile scope:** Shared SaaS using Shared Cell is selected for initial synthetic development and testing. Dedicated Managed and Dedicated Sovereign are deferred for independent profile evidence; compatible business semantics and isolation in all profiles, no cross-profile acceptance inference. Preserve Sovereign local authority and no mandatory synchronous Central dependency.
- **CM1-PD-98 — Conditional roadmap branches:** Gateway/BFF and Feature Eligibility included for integrated product path; Quota deferred unless mandatory platform obligation; Operational Aggregation deferred, baseline health monitoring preserved; Durable Scheduling deferred unless selected operation needs it; Numeric Objectives required to be determined during design/testing; In-App Notification and Approved Reports excluded from CM-1; Attachments included specifically for Customer evidence. No roadmap changes, waiver, or automatic execution authorization.
- **CM1-PD-99 — Dynamic Customer Document Catalog:** Tenant-configurable document types, no code release needed for additions. Stable document ID and fixed unique document code; Persian title, optional English title, description, applicability PERSON/COMPANY/BOTH, mandatory/optional stage, single/multiple files, permitted formats, maximum size within platform bounds, expiry-date applicability, ACTIVE/INACTIVE, display order. Example document types are illustrative, not global obligations. Exact code grammar/namespace, configuration storage and platform file limits OPEN.
- **CM1-PD-100 — Document requirement stage and change policy:** Tenant can require a document at initial registration or identity verification, or leave optional; default no mandatory initial registration documents. Add mandatory type or change optional to mandatory for subsequent applicable operations; mandatory to optional removes obligation; deactivate prevents new uploads for that type while preserving existing documents; rename retains identity; no physical deletion of type. Configuration changes do not automatically invalidate prior verification. Actual policy/legal prerequisites remain applicable.
- **CM1-PD-101 — Evidence upload and metadata:** Include authorized upload/view of one or multiple identity/company registration files as configured; record type, files, document number if needed, issue date, expiry when applicable, issuer, notes, uploader, upload date and version history. Replacement preserves prior versions. Upload alone does not set VERIFIED; independent authorized manual review with evidence and SoD still required. No OCR, automatic authenticity checking, external verification, general document-management or independent per-document approval workflow; expiry notifications deferred. File security and protected access mandatory; real evidence prohibited in initial synthetic development.
- **CM1-PD-102 — Document state and completeness:** Show NOT_PROVIDED, PROVIDED, EXPIRED, SUPERSEDED, INVALIDATED. PROVIDED denotes upload, not authenticity. INVALIDATED by authorized user; replacement retains historical SUPERSEDED version. Show completed mandatory documents versus total applicable mandatory documents, separately from verification status. Exact computation for stage, expiry/invalidity, zero denominator and precedence remains OPEN where not expressly settled; no new verification shortcuts.
- **CM1-PD-103 — Document authority and Audit:** Independent authority for catalog management, upload/replacement/customer-document management, and view/download. Catalog, upload, replacement and other mutations and access to sensitive documents require Audit; no automatic rights from other responsibilities. Preserve durable Audit and evidence minimization constraints. File storage, malware controls, authorization mappings, lifecycle/retention and recovery contracts remain OPEN.
- **CM1-PD-104 — Date and time:** Tenant-configurable timezone; initial Iran timezone Asia/Tehran. Relationship business dates are calendar days in Tenant timezone, half-open [start,end), future starts/open ends permitted. Issue/expiry of documents are calendar dates; date-only facts do not shift with timezone. Events and Audit timestamps UTC; user-local presentation and Persian/Gregorian calendar configurable independently of language. Preserve past business dates when Tenant timezone changes, and event history unchanged. Exact timezone-change effective evaluation and storage mechanisms OPEN.
- **CM1-PD-105 — Field validation:** Company name and Branch name required 1–255 characters; Person given/family names required 1–100 each; English name optional max 255; address max 1000, notes max 4000. Unicode, trim leading/trailing whitespace; preserve original display and normalized search/match representation. Phone supports domestic/international numbers, email structure, website HTTP/HTTPS, country catalog, province/city and postal validation per country. Optional fields validated if supplied; no uniqueness of names/phone/email. Backend validation mandatory. Exact character-count/normalization, country dataset and official identifier legal formats OPEN; structural validation is not identity proof.
- **CM1-PD-106 — Multiple contact channels and addresses:** Permit multiple phones, emails and addresses; allow designation of primary phone, primary email and primary address, and contact channel type. Removal/deactivation preserves required history and Audit. No new mandatory contact or address; exact primary replacement, contact type vocabulary and retention mechanisms OPEN.
